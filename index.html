02 / PROJECT BACKGROUND

How did Habitat 67 come to be?

1961

Thesis

Safdie develops a housing concept around modular construction and the qualities of a house.

1964

Development

The project moves from academic proposal toward a buildable architectural system.

1967

Expo 67

Habitat 67 is realized as a major residential experiment for Montréal's World Exposition.

The building should be read first as a response to a housing question, and only then as an architectural icon.

03 / ARCHITECT + DESIGN CONTEXT

Moshe Safdie

Safdie's early work focused on the possibility of combining the privacy and outdoor qualities of a house with the density and shared infrastructure of urban housing.

How can high-density housing retain qualities normally associated with a house?

05 / DESIGN IDEAS

Village, garden, community

01

Village / hillside

The stepped aggregation produces a form closer to a hillside settlement than a conventional apartment wall.

02

Garden

Each dwelling can receive an outdoor terrace through the relationship between one module and the module below.

03

Community

Pedestrian streets and shared spaces connect individual homes within a larger collective structure.
