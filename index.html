THE IDEA

A home in the city
without giving up the garden.

Habitat 67 proposed a different relationship between density and domestic life. Rather than a conventional apartment slab or tower, individual homes are assembled into a stepped collective form.

“Each apartment becomes an individual home within a larger collective organization.”— Safdie Architects

THE MODULE

One box.
Many possibilities.

The project is organized around prefabricated concrete boxes. The completed complex uses 365 construction modules to form 158 residences.

PRIVATE

Every home
gets outside.

Stepped modules create roof terraces that function as private gardens for the residences below.

COMMON

Every home
belongs to a neighborhood.

Pedestrian streets, play areas and shared circulation create communal space within the dense structure.

STRUCTURE

The building
holds itself together.

Units, pedestrian streets and elevator cores participate as load-carrying members. Connections include post-tensioning, high-tension rods, cables and welding.
